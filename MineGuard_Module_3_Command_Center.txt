MINEGUARD AI
MODULE 3 – DIGITAL MINE MAP, ALERTS, RESCUE AND ADMINISTRATION
1. Module Overview
Project: AI-Enabled Low-Cost Real-Time Mine Subsidence Monitoring, Prediction and Early Warning System with Smart Miner Safety BandModule Name: Digital Mine Map, Central Alert Management, Rescue Coordination and AdministrationAssigned To: Team Member 3Purpose: Build the command-center software that connects the outputs of Modules 1 and 2, visualizes the mine, manages alerts and incidents, coordinates rescue actions, and provides authentication and reporting.
2. Authentication and Roles
Administrator
Full system access, users, devices, mines, zones and configuration.
Control Room Operator
Live monitoring, alerts, mine map, miner monitoring and emergency communication.
Rescue Team
Active incidents, miner status, rescue updates and emergency information.
3. Digital Mine Map
Display mine structure and configurable zones.
Display sensor nodes from Module 1.
Display miners/bands using assigned or last known zone/location.
Color-code zones and devices as SAFE, WARNING or CRITICAL.
Show active SOS and emergency events.
Allow clicking a node, miner or zone to open details.
4. Central Alert Management
Receive AI/sensor alerts from Module 1.
Receive SOS, fall and band status alerts from Module 2.
Normalize all alerts into one central alert model.
Assign severity: INFO, WARNING or CRITICAL.
Support states: NEW, ACKNOWLEDGED, IN_PROGRESS and RESOLVED.
Push real-time notifications to dashboard and relevant users.
5. Rescue Coordination
Create incident from critical alerts when required.
Assign rescue team.
Show affected mine zone and miners.
Track incident status.
Store incident updates and final resolution.
6. Rescue Workflow
NEW INCIDENT→ ACKNOWLEDGED→ RESCUE TEAM ASSIGNED→ RESCUE IN PROGRESS→ MINER SAFE / RESOLVED→ INCIDENT CLOSED
7. Reports and Analytics
Sensor event report using data from Module 1.
Miner safety report using data from Module 2.
Incident report.
Alert history report.
Filter by mine, zone and date range.
